Passenger Fare & Timetable Guidelines

All routes, timings, and ticket fares displayed on this portal are authorized directly by the Valley Transit HP depot administration. Fares are calculated in accordance with state-approved mountain terrain passenger tariffs. In the event of unforeseen inclement weather, landslides, or road blocks, departure timings may be rescheduled at depot discretion.
