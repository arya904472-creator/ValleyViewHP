Mountain Highway & Long Route Guidelines

Long distance travel through high altitude passes (such as Rohtang, Jalori, or Kunzum passes) is subject to seasonal clearances and BRO road clearance updates. Passengers on overnight long routes are advised to report to the departure platform 20 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
